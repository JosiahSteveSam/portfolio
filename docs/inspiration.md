# Inspiration

Three sites Josiah picked. Notes taken 2026-11-01 from screenshots of each.

## 1. Format Bureau — https://s7zj4ixuvbxfi.ok.kimi.link/

A two-person graphic design studio (fictional, AI-built demo).

- Warm off-white paper with thin vertical column lines visible across the whole page.
- Hero: very heavy, high-contrast serif headline over three lines, a short paragraph, a large "Folio 2026" set in the same serif at the right.
- Projects placed on grid coordinates ("C1:R1", "C6:R2"), each with a type line (magazine, publisher) and one result. Black-and-white photos of the work.
- Monospace for nav and metadata; one red dot marks the active nav item.
- Sections numbered 01–05.
- **Take:** the paper, the visible grid, projects as indexed entries with one result each, numbered sections, the red-dot restraint.
- **Leave:** tracked-capital labels, "Zürich — New York · Est. 2019" meta strings, content hidden until scroll.

## 2. Recto — https://download.html.design/demo/recto-photography-portfolio-template/index.html

A photography portfolio template (preview page: https://html.design/preview/?theme=recto-photography-portfolio-template).

- White paper, large light serif headline ("The work of looking twice"), a small mono "Vol. 42" beside it.
- A row of colophon-style facts under the nav: volume, series, camera, issue, date.
- Thin horizontal rules between rows; italic serif for a quoted caption.
- Work presented as an archive of numbered series.
- **Take:** the archive / journal framing (volume, issue, numbered entries), the colophon row of facts, thin rules, the serif.
- **Leave:** tracked mono capitals, the single italic word in "Latest *six* series", arrows glued to links.

## 3. Roman Caseres — https://romancaseres.cloud/

A software developer's personal portfolio.

- Warm off-white paper. Huge heavy uppercase sans for "Software developer" and the name.
- His photo in the hero, beside the name.
- Giant role words between sections ("Web Dev & App Dev", "UI & Systems").
- Featured work as a scroll-scrubbed list with laptop mockups.
- **Take:** the personal photo, the name set huge in a heavy sans, the confidence of scale.
- **Leave:** the tech-logo grid in About, the scrolling ticker of tool names, laptop mockups, all-caps everywhere.
